## 1. 🤝 GENERAL COMMUNITY STANDARDS

* **1.1. Mutual Respect & Zero Tolerance for Harassment**  
  Treat all members, developers, staff, and server hosts with courtesy. Hate speech, discrimination, racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, xenophobia, personal attacks, bullying, and toxic behavior are strictly prohibited.

* **1.2. Family-Friendly & Safe Environment (SFW Only)**  
  This server is strictly **Safe for Work (SFW)**. Do not post, upload, or link any NSFW, sexually explicit, gory, violent, drug-related, or illegal content. This includes profile pictures, banners, usernames, custom statuses, and attachments.

* **1.3. Privacy & Anti-Doxxing Policy**  
  Do not share, request, or threaten to release private personal information (doxxing), real names, IP addresses, physical locations, phone numbers, social media profiles, or private conversations without explicit mutual consent.

* **1.4. Impersonation**  
  Impersonating Divine Client staff, developers, bots, Mojang/Microsoft representatives, or other server members will result in an immediate permanent ban.

* **1.5. Clean Language & Chat Flooding**  
  Keep swearing to a minimum and never direct insults at others. Flooding the chat with repetitive messages, character spam, wall-of-text ASCII art, all-caps yelling, or Zalgo text is not permitted.

## 2. 🔐 LAUNCHER SECURITY & ACCOUNT LINKING

* **2.1. Mandatory Discord Account Linking**  
  Divine Client v4.0.0 requires an active, verified Discord account connection to open the launcher, access cloud instances, synchronize friends, and operate server panels. Attempting to bypass the authentication gateway is a violation of the Terms of Service.

* **2.2. Keep Authorization Codes Confidential**  
  Your launcher pairing code and Discord verification tokens belong strictly to you. **Divine Staff will NEVER ask for your passwords, Microsoft session tokens, or private authorization codes.** Never share screenshots showing sensitive tokens.

* **2.3. No Account Sharing, Trading, or Selling**  
  Trading, selling, buying, or transferring Divine Client accounts, redemption codes, supporter perks, or linked profiles is strictly forbidden.

* **2.4. Multiple Accounts & Ban Evasion**  
  Using alternate Discord accounts to evade mutes, kicks, warnings, or device restrictions will trigger an automated permanent Hardware ID (HWID) blacklist across all Divine services.

## 3. ⚔️ FAIR PLAY, MODDING & SECURITY POLICY

* **3.1. Prohibited Cheats & Malicious Software**  
  The discussion, distribution, or promotion of hacked clients, unauthorized injected cheat modules, botting tools, x-ray bypasses, or cracked game files is strictly forbidden.

* **3.2. Custom Mods & Plugin Safety**  
  Only download mods, modpacks, loaders (Fabric, Forge, Quilt, Paper, Purpur), and plugins through the official Divine Client Modrinth/CurseForge browser or verified developer repositories. Distributing malicious `.jar` files, rat/malware downloaders, or token grabbers will result in immediate legal and hardware-level blacklisting.

* **3.3. Server Hosting Etiquette**  
  If hosting dedicated servers via Divine Server Host, you are responsible for maintaining a safe environment on your instance. Do not host server files configured for malicious intent (e.g. DDoS amplification, crypto-mining, or illegal content distribution).

## 5. 🔊 VOICE CHANNEL (VC) ETIQUETTE

* **5.1. Audio Quality & Courtesy**  
  Use push-to-talk or adjust your microphone noise suppression if you have loud background noise or keyboard click feedback.

* **5.2. No Ear-Rape or Soundboard Spam**  
  Screaming into the mic, playing ear-rape sounds, or spamming soundboards without explicit agreement from everyone in the voice channel is prohibited.

* **5.3. Recording & Streaming**  
  Inform everyone in the channel before recording voice conversations or streaming when private matters are discussed.

## 6. 🚫 ADVERTISING, LINKS & SOLICITATIONS

* **6.1. No Direct Message (DM) Advertising**  
  Sending unsolicited advertisements, server invites, services, or sales pitches via Direct Message to members of this server is an automatic, non-appealable ban.

* **6.2. No Unapproved Server Invites in Public Chats**  
  Discord server invite links and third-party commercial promotions are not allowed in public text channels without prior staff authorization.

* **6.3. Suspicious Links & Shorteners**  
  Posting malicious links, IP loggers, grabify links, URL shorteners that obscure destinations, or suspicious giveaways is strictly forbidden.

## 7. 🐛 BUG REPORTS, TECHNICAL SUPPORT & LOGS

* **7.1. How to Request Support**  
  When seeking assistance, clearly specify:
  1. Operating System (Windows 10/11, macOS, Linux).
  2. Divine Client Version (e.g., `v4.0.0`).
  3. Minecraft & Loader Version (e.g., `1.20.4 Fabric` or `1.20.4 Paper`).
  4. Allocated RAM & Java Version.
  5. Detailed error description and steps to reproduce.

* **7.2. Safe Log Uploading**  
  Always paste logs using reputable paste sites (such as [mclo.gs](https://mclo.gs/) or pastebin) or attach the sanitized `latest.log` file. Never paste logs containing authorization bearer tokens.

## 8. ⚖️ STAFF AUTHORITY, ENFORCEMENT & APPEALS

* **8.1. Staff Discretion**  
  Divine Client Moderators and Administrators retain the right to take necessary moderation actions (verbal warning, message deletion, timeout/mute, kick, temporary ban, or permanent hardware ban) to maintain server safety, even in situations not explicitly enumerated in these rules.

* **8.2. Moderation Progression**  
  - 🟡 **1st Offense:** Formal Warning / Message Deletion.
  - 🟠 **2nd Offense:** Temporary Mute / Timeout (1h - 24h).
  - 🔴 **3rd Offense:** Temporary Ban / Server Kick (1 - 7 days).
  - ⛔ **Severe / Malicious Offense:** Immediate Permanent Discord Ban & Divine Client Hardware ID (HWID) Blacklist.

* **8.3. Ban Appeals**  
  If you believe you received a moderation action or device restriction in error, submit a formal appeal through our official support ticket desk in `#appeals` or on our web portal at [https://divineclient.wispbyte.org/link](https://divineclient.wispbyte.org/link). Do not create alternate accounts to argue with staff.
