# 🏗️ Builder Studio Tools

Divine Client includes a dedicated **Builder Mode (1.21.11)** engineered specifically for mapmakers, architects, and cinematic creators.

---

## 📦 Integrated Builder Mods

When launching the **Builder** instance, Divine Client preloads:

1. **Axiom**: Modern, visual in-game building suite featuring real-time voxel painting, sculpt tools, smooth camera movements, and non-destructive transformations.
2. **WorldEdit (Fabric)**: Standard command-based world manipulation tool (`//set`, `//wand`, `//schem`, `//copy`, `//paste`).
3. **Flashback**: Instant in-game replay recorder and cinematic camera controller without external software dependencies.
4. **Divine Client Mod**: Full HUD and cosmetic suite integrated alongside builder tools.

---

## 🎨 Axiom Quick Start

1. Launch **Builder (1.21.11)** from the Divine Client launcher.
2. Enter a Singleplayer Creative world.
3. Press <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>B</kbd> or click the **Builder** button on the pause menu to open the Axiom UI.
4. Use the hotbar to switch between:
   - **Voxel Sculpting / Brush Tool**
   - **Selection Box / Transform Gizmo**
   - **Block Replace & Palette Blend**
