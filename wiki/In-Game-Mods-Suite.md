# 🧩 In-Game Mods Suite

Divine Client includes a built-in suite of performance optimizations and competitive HUD elements built directly into the client mod (`DivineClientMod-1.0.0.jar`).

---

## 🚀 Performance & Optimization

- **Sodium / Lithium Acceleration**: Zero-cost rendering optimizations boosting framerates and reducing frame drops.
- **Memory Optimization**: Efficient garbage collection flags and asset caching for smooth world rendering.
- **Dynamic Render Distance**: Smart chunk loading preventing CPU spikes during fast movement.

---

## 📊 HUD Elements

Access the HUD configuration menu by pressing <kbd>Right Shift</kbd> in-game.

### 1. FPS & CPS Counter
- Displays current real-time frames per second and Left/Right mouse clicks per second.
- Fully customizable text color, background transparency, and screen position.

### 2. Keystrokes Overlay
- Real-time display of `W`, `A`, `S`, `D`, `Space`, `LMB`, and `RMB` presses.
- Responsive color highlights when keys are pressed.

### 3. Armor & Status HUD
- Displays current durability and damage percentage for Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Boots, and Hand items.
- Active potion effect durations and amplifier levels.

### 4. Custom Crosshairs & Freelook
- Create custom static or dynamic crosshairs with custom colors and shapes.
- 360° perspective freelook (<kbd>F6</kbd>) for situational awareness.

### 5. Cosmetics & Wardrobe
- Dynamic capes, wings, bandanas, and hats rendered directly in-game.
- Real-time synchronization with your linked Discord profile.
